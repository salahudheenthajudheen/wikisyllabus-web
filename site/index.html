    How it's different

    Most AI tools answer questions.
    Your council teaches your course.

    §Knows your syllabus

    Every council is built from your university's actual syllabus: modules, scope and outcomes. It tells you when something is out of syllabus. See the source.

    ↻Remembers how you learn

    Bring in your AI's memory to start, and the council keeps a learning profile: weak topics, repeat mistakes, how you like things explained. Each week it hands you an update to save back.

    ◎Lives in your AI

    No new app, no account. Your council is a set of open skill files you add to Gemini or Claude. Nothing is sent to us. There is no “us” server.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier. The skill files are plain text you can read before you upload them.

    Which AI can I use it with?

    Any AI that supports skill files (the open SKILL.md format): Gemini and Claude today, and ChatGPT on Business, Enterprise and Edu plans. On ChatGPT's free plan you can put the pack into a Project instead.

    How does it remember me?

    Your pack includes a learning profile. Paste your AI's memory once and it keeps only the study-related facts. At the end of each week it writes a short “learning memory” for you to save back into your AI, so every chat knows how you learn, not just council chats.

    Will it do my assignments for me?

    No. Your council is built to make you understand, not to hand in work for you. It will explain, quiz and critique, but it won't write graded submissions.

    Is the exam pattern accurate?

    Your council won't make up an exam pattern. The first time you prepare for an exam it asks you to paste a past paper or your exam format, then sets questions in that style.

    My university or course isn't listed.

    Add its syllabus to the WikiSyllabus repo as a markdown file. Once it's merged, it shows up here and gets a council like every other course.

    Does WikiSyllabus collect any data?

    No. This page runs in your browser and builds your pack there. Your conversations stay in your own AI account.
